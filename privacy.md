# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

## Personal use

Local Personal Assistant is a private application operated by Ian for personal use. It is not offered as a public service.

## Google data accessed

The application reads Google Calendar information. Its synchronization component searches Gmail for work calendar snapshot emails and reads their JSON attachments.

It creates and manages a dedicated Work Mirror calendar, adding, updating, or removing mirrored events when the source calendar changes.

Although Gmail authorization permits reading other messages, the synchronization component is configured to retrieve work calendar snapshot messages.

## Use and storage

Google data is used to provide personal calendar assistance and synchronize work events. Calendar information provided to the assistant is processed locally using Ollama.

Authorization tokens and synchronization settings are stored locally on the owner’s Mac. Mirrored events are stored in the owner’s Google Calendar. Snapshot emails remain in Gmail unless the owner removes them.

## Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Data passes through Google services and the owner’s configured Microsoft Power Automate email workflow as needed to perform the synchronization.

The application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Control and deletion

The owner can stop synchronization, revoke access through Google Account settings, delete locally stored authorization and synchronization files, and remove snapshot emails and the Work Mirror calendar.
